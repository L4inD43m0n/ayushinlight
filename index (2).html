Quotes and thoughts I’ve gathered along the way. More on Instagram and Threads.

    It’s okay to fail and make mistakes. We’re still young, and still learning, growing.

  • 02

    You can always return to a place, but you can never return to the moment you once lived here. The same streets may still be there, the same walls may still remember you, but somehow, everything feels different. The people have grown, life has moved on, and you have become someone new. Maybe that’s the quiet ache of growing up.

  • 03

    Life gets better when you realize there are no wrong decisions, because every choice leads you exactly where you’re meant to be.

  • 04

    Please live your life the way you want because people will always have something to say.

  • 05

    Somewhere, someone still remembers you because, at a time when they felt invisible, your kindness made them feel seen.

  • 06

    The highest form of peace is having no desire to be seen, validated or understood by anyone.

    The curse of discipline is that every day looks the same. The curse of comfort is that every year looks the same.

  • 09

    One day you realize that the little moments with family were actually the biggest moments of your life.
